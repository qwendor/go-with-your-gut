Background
Pepsi is struggling to connect with older audiences and needs a marketing strategy to reach those consumers, while still engaging its core audience of younger adults. These consumers are looking for a way to incorporate healthier soft drinks.
Problem
Older generations are increasingly health conscious, meaning they want something with less caffeine and sugar.
Objective
Bring greater awareness to Pepsi's health products, such as prebiotic sodas.
Audience
Health-conscious adults 65+
Older adults who want to be healthier but struggle with cravings.
Message
A treat can still be healthy.
Insight
You can crave something that's good for you.
Strategy
Position this product as a healthy alternative to regular Pepsi, taking away the guilt older consumers feel about drinking soda that is high in sugar and lacks nutritional value.
